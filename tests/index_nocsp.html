Lifetime Wrapped

Drop in the listening history Spotify gave you and see every year of it: your top songs, the song that ruled each month, when you listen, what you keep coming back to, and more.

Your data stays on your computer

  • The file is read by this page, in your browser. It is never uploaded. There is no server behind this page to receive it.
  • Nothing is stored: no database, no cookies, no analytics. Close the tab and it is gone.
  • The page is blocked from making any network connection by its own security policy, so it could not send your data out even by mistake.
  • Only song, artist, album and play length are read. IP address, country and device in your file are ignored.
  • You can check: open your browser's developer tools, look at the Network tab while you load a file, and watch nothing leave. The code is a few plain files with no outside libraries.

Where do I get the file?

  1. Sign in at spotify.com and open Account, then Privacy settings.
  2. Under Download your data, tick Extended streaming history and request it.
  3. Spotify emails you a link, which can take days to arrive. Download the zip and drop it above.

The shorter "Account data" download only covers the last year, so it will show far less.
