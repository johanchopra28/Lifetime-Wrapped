How to get your file

Spotify has to prepare it, so ask for it now.

Open privacy settings

Sign in at spotify.com, then Account, then Privacy settings.

Ask for your history

Under Download your data, tick Extended streaming history and request it.

Drop the zip above

Spotify emails a link (it can take days). Download the zip and drop it here.

The shorter "Account data" download only covers the last year, so it shows far less.

Your data stays on your computer

  • The file is read by this page, in your browser. There is no server behind it to receive your history, and nothing is uploaded.
  • Nothing is saved: no database, no cookies, no analytics. Close the tab and it is gone.
  • Only the song, artist, album and play length are read. Your IP address, country and device in the file are ignored.
  • The page's security policy blocks all network connections except an optional album-cover lookup. That lookup is off by default, and when you switch it on, only the Spotify IDs of the songs on screen go to Spotify.
  • Check it yourself: open your browser's developer tools, watch the Network tab while you load a file, and nothing leaves. The code is a handful of plain files, with no outside libraries.
